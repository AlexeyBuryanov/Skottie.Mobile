# Code of Conduct

## A welcoming community

Skottie.Mobile welcomes people of all backgrounds and experience levels. Everyone
participating in the project should be able to ask questions, contribute, and
disagree without facing harassment or discrimination.

Treat others with respect regardless of age, disability, ethnicity, nationality,
race, religion, gender identity or expression, sexual orientation, appearance,
education, or professional experience.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate and patient, especially with people who are learning.
- Discuss the technical merits of a proposal without attacking its author.
- Give specific, constructive feedback and listen to other perspectives.
- Respect people's time, boundaries, and decisions about participation.
- Acknowledge mistakes, correct them, and help repair any harm caused.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, stalking, threats, intimidation, or discriminatory remarks.
- Sexual harassment or unwanted sexual attention, language, or imagery.
- Personal insults, deliberate provocation, or repeated disruption of discussions.
- Sharing someone's private information without their permission.
- Retaliation against someone who raises a concern or assists with a report.
- Continuing unwanted contact or evading a moderation restriction.

## Scope

This policy applies to the repository's issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions,
and other project spaces. It also applies when someone officially represents
Skottie.Mobile in another public space. Maintainers and contributors are held to
the same standards.

## Reporting concerns

For incidents on GitHub, use the **Report content** option on the relevant issue,
pull request, or comment. Choose **Report to repository admins** when GitHub offers
that option. If it is unavailable, or the concern involves a repository
administrator, use **Report abuse to GitHub Support** or
[GitHub's abuse reporting form](https://support.github.com/contact/report-abuse).
See [GitHub's reporting instructions](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam)
for available reporting methods.

Include links, a description of what happened, and relevant context. Avoid opening
public issues that expose personal information or sensitive incident details.
Reports sent to GitHub Support are handled by GitHub; they are not a private
message to the project maintainer.

## How maintainers respond

Maintainers review concerns brought to their attention fairly and consider the
severity, context, and history of the behavior. They should limit disclosure of
report details to what is needed to address the incident and protect participants.

Depending on the situation, maintainers may ask for a correction, issue a warning,
remove inappropriate content, restrict participation temporarily, or permanently
exclude a participant. Serious threats or harassment may warrant immediate
restriction. Where appropriate, maintainers will explain the reason and scope of
a moderation decision to the affected participant.

Do not use another account or ask someone else to bypass a restriction. If you
believe a moderation decision is mistaken, provide relevant context through the
same reporting channel rather than restarting the dispute in public threads.
